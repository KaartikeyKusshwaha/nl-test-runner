# name: PHPTravels Dubai hotel search
# url: https://phptravels.net/stays
# expect: pass
Dismiss the informational demo notice if it blocks the page. Click the Destination or Hotel Name field and type Dubai in Search By City. Wait for the suggestions, then click the option named Dubai marked Destination. Do not press Enter or move to another field until that city option is selected. Open Nationality, type India in Search country..., and choose the suggestion whose full country name is exactly "India"; do not choose "British Indian Ocean Territory". Keep the default guests and dates, then click Search Hotels. Verify the results URL contains "/stays/dubai/" and "/IN/" and the exact message "No Stays found" is visible. Do not open a hotel or submit payment.
